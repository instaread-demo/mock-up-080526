Audi A2 will return as an electric model later this year

  • By Matt De Prez
  • |
  • 4 August 2026
  • News

Audi is gearing up to re-launch the A2 in the autumn as it expands its electric car line-up.

The A2 e-tron sits on the same platform as the VW ID3 and will go on sale as an electric alternative to the current A3.

Drawing on the heritage of the original A2, the new model brings similar styling cues and promises class-leading efficiency.

The body has been designed to minimise aerodynamic drag, while a new motor has been developed to reduce losses.

Testing is already underway, with Audi claiming that 4.85mi/kWh is possible from the new hatchback.

The A2 e-tron will use a 61kWh battery and a 190PS motor. It should be capable of around 300 miles from a single charge.

The new pack uses lithium iron phosphate (LFP) cells, which Audi claims can be charged to 100% without degrading battery life.

It's likely that other battery and motor configurations will be available, in due course.

More details on the new A2 e-tron will be available when the car is officially unveiled in the autumn.
