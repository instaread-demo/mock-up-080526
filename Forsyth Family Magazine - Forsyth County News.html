Weeks Hardwood Flooring – Style That Suits Your Family

  • Martie Emory
  • August 1, 2026
photos by:  JODIE BRIM CREATIVE

Presenting the largest showroom for prefinished hardwood, custom hardwood flooring and site-finished hardwood flooring in the Piedmont Triad, Weeks Hardwood Flooring has been treating loyal clients as trusted friends since 1979.

While their spacious new showroom address may say Greensboro, the Weeks team is a mere 20 minutes from downtown Winston-Salem, conveniently situated at the intersection of Highway 68 and I-40. They have served clients in more than 20 zip codes, expanding the Weeks promise of quality and craftsmanship far and wide.

Vice President Cameron Weeks shares that, since debuting the incredible new showroom earlier this year, the family owned business now has expanded office space, a showroom that’s triple its previous size and the ability to maintain and streamline both warehousing and onsite work all in one place.

Another exciting feature you’ll notice right away when you visit, 51 large display panels of prefinished flooring on the walls right when you walk in the showroom door. 

“We’re thrilled our customers can more thoroughly examine our top-tier flooring products in person, while better appreciating all of each option’s color variation and character,” Cameron shares.

Founded by Cameron’s father, James Weeks, Weeks Hardwood Flooring is a business rooted in the strongest work ethic you’ll find anywhere, driven by a dedicated team who backs every single project with their very best efforts. 

Longtime clients know the Weeks Hardwood Flooring team wants you to appreciate your experience with them for many years to come! Loyal repeat clients, along with buyers of new construction who have heard the praise and witnessed the Weeks reputation throughout the industry, affirm that Cameron and Operations Manager Greg Spurlin have built a team of skilled, professionally trained, in-house work crews. Those are consummate professionals you’ll always feel comfortable having work inside your home.

Add to that a promise of utilizing only top-of-the-line products, and you’ll see why clients consistently applaud this respected family owned business for its excellent workmanship, fair pricing and punctuality – making your flooring project a seamless task from start to finish. The Weeks team is also known for expertly matching existing flooring and both repairing and refinishing wood floors in homes across the region.

If carpeting is the cozy solution you’re seeking for your home, Weeks Hardwood Flooring also offers precision cut rugs custom tailored to how your family lives. It’s easy to request a free in-home consultation right from their website. And, if you aren’t able to immediately visit the showroom, you can always order a flooring sample online to be shipped right to your home. Or, upload a photo of your space on their website, and see just how a particular color will look in your actual room. The options are endless!

“Trend wise, we are seeing more brown tones and more color in homes than just completely whites and neutrals,” shares Cameron. “We’re also seeing the popularity of floors with distinctive character (knots or other obvious variations) such as walnut spirals.”

The craftsmanship you’re seeing in the showroom today is inspired by nature – everything from oceans to canyons – delivering comfort, style and timeless appeal.

“People tend to want their home to be an extension of the natural area around where they live,” adds Cameron. “You’ll see different looks for coastal regions versus the mountains, and in our Piedmont Triad region, more natural colors. We’re showing off the best in North Carolina living!”

Weeks Hardwood Flooring offers residential services in the Greater Greensboro/Winston-Salem/High Point area along with commercial services across North Carolina, South Carolina and Virginia. Whether you’re a homeowner, contractor, developer or property manager, this savvy, experienced team is able to proudly serve all your flooring needs with honest communication from the first estimate through the final installation process.

 

Visit the Weeks Hardwood Flooring team in their amazing showroom at 7901 Thorndike Road in Greensboro, or call them at 336.856.0202 to arrange your free in-home consultation and estimate. Follow them online at weekshardwoodflooring.com and on Facebook and Instagram @weekshardwoodflooring for news, product updates and style inspiration for your home! Showroom hours are Monday through Friday 8:00 a.m. – 5:00 p.m. and Saturday 9:00 a.m. – 1:00 p.m.

“Weeks did a wonderful, fast and efficient job installing a carpet stair runner in our home!” ~ Beth B.

“I will always trust Weeks Flooring with my projects. The crews were expert, diligent and cared about achieving excellent results. They were all also very polite. Having a continued mess for a month – consisting of two complicated phases of installation – was stressful, but the crews made it as easy as possible. They cared about getting the same color of flooring upstairs as we had downstairs, despite the challenge of varying light levels in the different spaces. I hope to have Weeks Flooring return next year to complete another project for me.” ~ Deborah B.
