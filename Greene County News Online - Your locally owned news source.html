4-H state fair selections told

August 2, 2026August 4, 2026

Judging of 2026 Greene County 4-H static exhibit entries took place Tuesday, July 7. A variety of subject matter experts judged in the areas of animals; agriculture and natural resources; creative arts; science, engineering, and technology; family and consumer sciences; and personal development. The static exhibit judging event was coordinated by static superintendents Stacy Hardaway, Michele Hardin, Shari Minnehan, and Linda Hedges.

Results of the 2026 Greene County 4-H static exhibit judging were as follows:
Iowa State Fair Selections:

CREATIVE ARTS: Becca Anderson, Pencil Drawing;  Cheyenne Clouse, Frame Jean Pocket; Becca Anderson, Pencil Color Drawing; Robert Tasler, Photography; Mylie Miller, Photography; and Ella Tasler, Photography (X4).

SCIENCE, ENGINEERING, & TECHNOLOGY: Harrison Smith, Turkey Display; Madison Christensen, End Table; Aden Bardole, Wooden Tree; and  Haley Scheuermann, Church Pew

FAMILY & CONSUMER SCIENCES: Jaelynn Hedges, Food stand; Savannah Lansman & Riah Riphagen, Healthy Snacks; Autumn Barrett, Quilt; Scarlett Graham, Table Runner; Riah Riphagen, Denim Bag and Dance Costume Display; Ava Allen, Handkerchief Wreath; Tayleigh Thorpe, Outdoor Décor; Kaliyah Minnihan, Cow print Mirror; Grace Black, Cherry Turnover; Natalie Towers, Chocolate Star Cookies; Becca Anderson, M&M Dream Bars; and  Savannah Lansman, Sheet Pan Quesadilla Thrifty Meal Challenge.

PERSONAL DEVELOPMENT: Riah Riphagen, Penny Art; Ella Tasler, Let your Light Shine Poster Project; and Aiden Marshall, Spool Refurbished Donation.

AG & NATURAL RESOURCES: Savannah Lansman, Rabbit Showmanship; and Kenna Minnihan, Beef Vs. Dairy Poster.

Outstanding 4th Grade Exhibits: Walker Barrett, fish and wildlife, and conservation, environment and sustainability; Cade Muir, animal science; Matylin McNeill, visual arts; Mackenzy McNeill, visual arts; Wyatt Christensen, tractor; Harper Lawton, self-determined and home improvement; Niyah Hoyt, child development and photography; Lee Holz, food and nutrition; Anna Thomas, visual arts; Mabel Schroeder, food and nutrition; Karson Marshall, home improvement; Levi Daniel, horticulture and plant science, and Harper Lawton, sewing and needle arts, woodworking, and citizenship and civic engagement.
