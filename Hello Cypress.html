Things To Do in August

By Hello Cypress | July 30, 2026

Hello Cypress’ “Things To Do” monthly guide highlights events, activities, and things to do this August in Cypress and Northwest Harris County area PLUS ways to give back and support local. Be sure to check back on Hello Cypress for updates and events!

August 2025

FEATURED EVENTS

Aug 1, 2026 – Back To School Drive-Thru Event – Cypress Assistance Ministries is hosting their Back to School Supply Event on Saturday, August 1st from 9 a.m. to 3 p.m., providing students in need with essential school supplies to start the year strong. Families in the Cypress area are invited to attend and receive backpacks and grade-appropriate items to help set their children up for academic success. It’s a day of support, community, and preparation for the upcoming school year! Learn More

Aug 1, 2026 – Back to School Supply Drive-Thru – Cy-Hope’s Back to School Supply Drive-Thru returns on August 1st, offering free backpacks and basic school supplies to families in need—no pre-registration required! Supplies will be distributed on a first-come, first-served basis, and students must be present in the car to receive a backpack. While each student will receive essential supplies, please note that not all items from school supply lists can be guaranteed. Plan to arrive early and help your student start the school year prepared! Learn More

Advertisement

August 1 – September 7, 2026 – Houston Restaurant Weeks – Houston Restaurant Weeks™ 2026 is a 38-day dining event benefiting the Houston Food Bank. From August 1 through September 7, participating restaurants across Greater Houston will offer specially priced prix fixe brunch, lunch, and dinner menus, with proceeds helping provide meals for those in need. Menus go live July 15—view participating restaurants and plan your dining experience at houstonrestaurantweeks.com.

Aug 2026 – Escapology Cypress is a unique, immersive, and always private escape game where players are gathered inside a themed room and must complete their mission before time runs out. Stepping inside a real-life adventure, they must find hidden clues, crack codes, and solve puzzles to make an escape—all in an hour! Learn More

August 28, 2026 – Cy-Fair Ten Pin Challenge – You’re invited to an exciting afternoon of bowling, food, and fun at the Cy-Fair Ten Pin Challenge on Friday, August 28! Grab your co-workers for a high-energy day of team building and rock & roll vibes at the lanes. A $425 team entry includes 4 bowlers, 3 games, shoe rental, and meal tickets for each participant. Hosted by the Cy-Fair Houston Chamber of Commerce and proudly underwritten by Houston Methodist Cypress Hospital, this is one event you won’t want to miss! Learn More

August 1 – September 7, 2026 – Dario’s Steakhouse & Seafood’s – Guests will delight in a refined multi-course experience at a great value, all while giving back to the Houston Food Bank. Located in Cypress, Dario’s will feature exclusive $55 dinner and $25 lunch offerings (plus tax and gratuity). The restaurants will donate $7 from each dinner and $3 from each lunch purchase from their special menus to support local hunger relief. Learn More

August 1 – September 7, 2026 – Marvino’s Italian Steakhouse – Guests will delight in a refined multi-course experience at a great value, all while giving back to the Houston Food Bank. Located in Cypress, Marvino’s will feature exclusive $55 dinner and $25 lunch offerings (plus tax and gratuity). The restaurants will donate $7 from each dinner and $3 from each lunch purchase from their special menus to support local hunger relief. Learn More

THINGS TO DO

Cypress‑Fairbanks ISD is gearing up for the new school year, officially kicking off on Wednesday, August 12, 2026. Parents and students can find everything they need in the comprehensive CFISD Back‑to‑School Guide, which includes essential information on new student registration, annual student updates via the Home Access Center (HAC), transportation services (My Ride K‑12), nutrition & free or reduced meal benefits, district calendar, immunization requirements, and other student support resources. Whether you’re a returning CFISD family or new to the district, the guide is your one-stop source to make sure your child starts the year confidently and prepared. View The Guide

Try out a new restaurant or revisit a favorite for a great cause during Houston Restaurant Weeks! Find out which local restaurants are participating in this annual fundraiser benefitting the Houston Food Bank, Montgomery County Food Bank, and Galveston County Food Bank. The month-long event runs from August 1 through September 7, 2026. View The Guide

Hello Cypress is serving up summer fun with our Hot Dog Guide. Your go-to list for the best hot dogs in town! From classic ballpark-style dogs to gourmet creations loaded with toppings, we’re highlighting local spots where you can satisfy your hot dog cravings. Whether you’re celebrating National Hot Dog Day or just looking for a quick bite, this guide has the must-try dogs you won’t want to miss! View The Guide

Welcome to the 2026 Summer Camp Guide for Cypress! Whether your child is passionate about sports, the arts, outdoor adventures, or academic enrichment, this guide has something for every interest and age group. Cypress is home to a variety of exciting camps that offer fun, learning, and new experiences, ensuring your child has an unforgettable summer. From creative workshops to active sports programs, discover the best local camps that will keep your kids engaged and thriving all season long! View the Guide

Advertisement

August 7, 2026 – 54rd Annual Tomball Night – Get ready for a night of fun, food, and family at the 54rd Annual Tomball Night on Friday, August 7th from 5 to 10 p.m. in Downtown Tomball! This beloved community tradition features live music, street vendors, kids’ activities, a lighted parade, and a spectacular fireworks show to close out the evening. Shops will stay open late, and local businesses will line the streets with giveaways, games, and fun for all ages. Don’t miss this unforgettable summer celebration! Learn More

August 8, 2026 – YMCA Langham Creek Melon & Berry Fest – Get ready for a juicy summer celebration at the Melon & Berry Festival hosted at the Cypress Farmers Market on Saturday, August 8, 2026, from 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. at 10519 Fry Rd, Cypress, TX. Featuring over 5,000 lbs of seasonal melons, berries, and Hill Country peaches, this vibrant event includes fresh-pressed juices, homemade jams and jellies, warm berry baked goods, and locally sourced meats and eggs. Browse local artisan vendors and handcrafted goods while enjoying music, family activities, and a welcoming community atmosphere. Perfect for all ages—come shop local, snack fresh, and savor summer’s best! Learn More

August 15, 2026 – Cheers for Charity benefiting Second Chances – Raise a glass for a great cause at Cheers for Charity, benefiting Second Chances, on August 15, 2026 from 4:00 to 7:00 p.m. at the Red Oak Ballroom at Norris Conference Centers. Guests will enjoy an evening of fabulous wines, delicious food, and community while supporting orphaned children around the world. Proceeds from the event help provide schools, clothing, clean water, and hope—with 100% of the proceeds going directly to the children. Join the community for an unforgettable evening where every sip makes a difference. Learn More

Advertisement

August 8, 2026 – Cypress Cars & Coffee – (8:00am – 10:00am) Open to the public to visit but show cars are required to register and requested to provide information in advance of the show. Visit their website and submit your vehicle year, make, and model for registration or DM us on FB or IG. Registration is FREE. Show cars are asked to remain parked until at least 930am. Harris County PCT 4 Constable Security will be onsite along with our coffee vendor Baby Bloo Coffee. We encourage families and auto enthusiasts to come see these unique and incredible machines! Learn More

August 9 and 23, 2026 – Cypress Farmers Market – Every 2nd & 4th Sunday – Health is wealth all year long at Your Neighborhood Farmers Market. Grab your family, friends, and shopping bags! Stop in at the signature 30ft organic produce table. Shop local businesses from Authentic Indian cuisine, Fredericksburg peaches, sauces & salsa, jams & jellies, cage free eggs, grass-fed meats, scratch-made breads & baked goods, handmade jewelry, handcrafted candles & scents, and one-of a kind artisan items. Learn More

August 15, 2026 – Tuckerton Village Melon & Berry Fest – Get ready for a juicy summer celebration at the Melon & Berry Festival hosted at the Tuckerton Village Farmers Market on Saturday, August 15, 2026, from 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. Featuring over 5,000 lbs of seasonal melons, berries, and Hill Country peaches, this vibrant event includes fresh-pressed juices, homemade jams and jellies, warm berry baked goods, and locally sourced meats and eggs. Browse local artisan vendors and handcrafted goods while enjoying music, family activities, and a welcoming community atmosphere. Perfect for all ages—come shop local, snack fresh, and savor summer’s best! Learn More

August 16, 2026 – Fairfield Melon & Berry Fest – BERRY exciting news! You’re invited to the sweetest, most flavorful event of the summer, the Melon & Berry Festival at the Fairfield Farmers Market on Sunday, August 16 from 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. at 15103 Mason Rd, Cypress. With over 5,000 lbs. of fresh strawberries, blueberries, raspberries, blackberries, juicy melons, and Hill Country peaches, it’s a fruity celebration you won’t want to miss! Enjoy homemade jams, fresh-pressed juices, baked goods, grass-fed meats, eggs, and handcrafted local goods. The event also features kids activities, music, food, artisan vendors, and is pet-friendly and wheelchair accessible. Come shop local, snack fresh, and make sweet memories with the whole family! Learn More

August 8 and 22, 2026 – YMCA Langham Creek Farmers Market – Every 2nd & 4th Saturday (10:00am – 2:00pm) – Health is wealth all year long at Your Neighborhood Farmers Market. Grab your family, friends, and shopping bags! Stop in at the signature 30ft organic produce table. Shop local businesses from Authentic Indian cuisine, Fredericksburg peaches, sauces & salsa, jams & jellies, cage free eggs, grass-fed meats, scratch-made breads & baked goods, handmade jewelry, handcrafted candles & scents, and one-of a kind artisan items and so much more. Learn More

August 15, 2026 – Towne Lake Farmer’s Market – Stop by The Boardwalk every third Saturday of the month from 11 a.m. to 3 p.m. for the Farmer’s Market with live music! Enjoy fresh fruit, vegetables, homemade goodies and more from local vendors. Stay for brunch, lunch or dinner! Learn More

Advertisement

August 1, 8, 15, 22 & 29, 2026 – Tomball Farmers Market – Every Saturday (9:00am-1:00pm) Tomball Farmers Market features over 70 local vendors including farmers, producers, bakers, artisans and more. Located in historic Downtown Tomball, the market provides a diverse, fun, pet friendly, family outdoor shopping experience. Dates: Saturdays, rain or shine, except for March 23, 2024 & December 14, 2024. Time: 9:00am–1:00pm. Address: 205 W Main St, Tomball, TX 77375. Learn More

August 29, 2026 – Cypress Melon & Berry Fest – Get ready for a juicy summer celebration at the Melon & Berry Festival hosted at the Cypress Farmers Market on Saturday, August 29, 2026, from 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. at 10519 Fry Rd, Cypress, TX. Featuring over 5,000 lbs of seasonal melons, berries, and Hill Country peaches, this vibrant event includes fresh-pressed juices, homemade jams and jellies, warm berry baked goods, and locally sourced meats and eggs. Browse local artisan vendors and handcrafted goods while enjoying music, family activities, and a welcoming community atmosphere. Perfect for all ages—come shop local, snack fresh, and savor summer’s best! Learn More

Save the date September 4-7, 2026 – Jurassic Quest at NRG Center – Step into a prehistoric adventure as Jurassic Quest returns to NRG Center! This exciting, family-friendly event features life-size, sky-scraping animatronic dinosaurs, one-of-a-kind walking dinosaur rides, and a life-size T. rex skull that will wow dino lovers of all ages. Visitors can explore incredible fossils, including real T. rex teeth and a Triceratops horn, while enjoying interactive exhibits, fossil digs, and hands-on science activities. Don’t miss this unforgettable journey back in time—grab your tickets and get ready for a roaring good time! Learn More

Save the date Oct. 3, 2026 – Cy-Fest – LSC-CyFair’s annual free family-friendly community celebration, set from 10 a.m. – 2 p.m. Saturday, Oct. 3, features a pumpkin patch, arts and crafts, children’s trains, inflatable, musical entertainment, STEM activities, food trucks and much more. Come meet firefighters and see fire trucks and equipment up close. Get information on the college’s Center for the Arts, Academy for Lifelong Learning, library services and other programs. Go to LoneStar.edu/cy-fest for the most current information, including sponsorship opportunities to showcase your business to thousands of visitors.
