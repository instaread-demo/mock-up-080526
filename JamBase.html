SiriusXM’s Grateful Dead Channel Welcomes Jim James As Special Guest DJ

The My Morning Jacket frontman will share stories and spin some of his favorite Grateful Dead recordings during SiriusXM’s annual “Days Between” programming.

By Scott Bernstein Aug 4, 2026 • 12:53 pm PDT

My Morning Jacket frontman Jim James will take over SiriusXM’s Grateful Dead Channel (Ch. 23) as special guest DJ for a special two-hour set. The singer-songwriter will guide listeners through his favorite Grateful Dead songs while discussing the band’s enduring influence.

James’ appearance comes as part of SiriusXM’s annual “Days Between” programming on the Grateful Dead Channel. The “Days Between,” August 1 – 9, marks the time between the anniversaries of Jerry Garcia's birth and his passing.

The set airs live on SiriusXM today, August 4 at 4 p.m. ET. Replays are scheduled for Thursday, August 6 at 8 a.m. ET, Saturday, August 8 at 12 a.m. ET (Friday Night) and later the same day at 3 p.m. ET.

“Don’t miss my set on The Grateful Dead’s SiriusXM Channel, where I’ll be paying tribute to our dearly departed members as well as exploring the magic, mystery, beauty, and wonder that is the Grateful Dead now, then, forever and again!” James wrote about his guest DJ gig on social media.

Jim James will release his first new solo album in eight years, Wowed Out, on August 28 through ATO Records. The Louisville native hits the road in late fall for a run of solo acoustic tour dates in support of the LP.
