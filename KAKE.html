SEDGWICK COUNTY, Kan. (KAKE) – A Wichita man has been found dead after being reported missing, officials said Tuesday night.

The Wichita Police Department posted a missing person alert on social media on Tuesday afternoon for 23-year-old Bryce Wilks of Wichita. 

According to the Sedgwick County Sheriff’s Office, deputies gathered information through cellphone technology that led officers to the area of 37th Street North and Hoover Road. There, officers located a crashed vehicle occupied by Wilks. He was pronounced dead at the scene. 

According to preliminary investigation, Wilks was driving eastbound on 37th Street North when he lost control of the vehicle and struck multiple trees. The sheriff’s office said his family has been notified, and the crash remains under investigation.
