Hot Country Nights Concert Series in the Power & Light District

POSTED BY
Teresa Mears

Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 1:09
Instaread

The Hot Country Nights Summer Concert Series is back this summer in the Kansas City Power & Light District every Thursday night through August 27, 2026.

The shows are no longer free, but with ticket prices of about $20 for most shows, they are way less than you would pay for many concerts. You must be 18 or older to attend, and some of the seating areas are open only to those 21-pus. 

The popular Thursday night series features nine concerts from July through August, showcasing a mix of rising stars and established country performers.

The  concerts start at 7 p.m.

2026 Coors Light Hot Country Nights schedule

  • July 2, Pecos & The Rooftops, 7 p.m.
  • July 9, Tyler Braden, 7 p.m.
  • July 16, Blake Whiten, 7 p.m.
  • July 23, Dylan Scott, 7 p.m.
  • July 30, Redferrin, 7 p.m.
  • August 6, Tyler Hubbard, 7 p.m.
  • August 13, Tucker Wetmore, 7 p.m.
  • August 20, The Band Perry, 7 p.m.
  • August 27, George Birge, 7 p.m.

You can get tickets here.

KC Live! is located at 1100 Walnut Street in Kansas City, Missouri.
