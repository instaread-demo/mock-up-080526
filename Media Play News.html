Samba: ‘House of the Dragon’ Tops Weekly Streaming Viewership Through July 26

John Latchem

August 2, 2026

Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 0:56
Instaread

HBO Max’s “House of the Dragon” remained No. 1 on Samba’s list of top streaming programs for the week ended July 26.

The third season of the “Game of Thrones” prequel about a family civil war for the throne returned to HBO and for streaming on HBO Max June 21.

Still No. 2 was Netflix’s new series “The Hawk,” starring Will Ferrell as a washed-up former golf champion who seeks to re-ignite his winning ways. It debuted July 16.

Up three spots to No. 3 was Prime Video’s action comedy series “Ride or Die,” starring and executive produced by Oscar-winner Octavia Spencer and Hannah Waddingham (“Ted Lasso”).  A Paramount Television Studios and Amazon MGM Studios production, the series debuted July 15. The storyline follows best friends Debbie Claybourne (Spencer) and Judith Burton (Waddingham), who thought they knew everything about each other. Then Judith turns out to be an international assassin.
