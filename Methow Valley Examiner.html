Images of Sinlahekin Fire as Winds and Dry Conditions Accelerate its Advance

The Sinlahekin Fire more than doubled in size from Saturday to Sunday to 73,794 acres. The cities of Oroville, Tonasket and Ellisforde are under Level 2 warning.

Highway 97 separates the town of Riverside from a Level 3 evacuation area.

Washington Department of Natural Resources reports the wildfire intensified due to gusty winds and a “very dry fire environment” with little relief in sight.

“With the upcoming cold front expected to carry wind gusts up to 40 mph, direct suppression efforts will likely be minimized while resources continue to focus on point protection, spotting, and new starts,” according to field notes by the department.

“Fire will continue to move toward the S/SE under a N/NW wind. This has the potential to impact Conconully, Lime Belt, Riverside, and the greater Highway 97 corridor.”

All images courtesy of Amanda Cunningham, Air Quality and Smoke Specialist with the Interagency Wildland Fire Air Quality Response Program.
