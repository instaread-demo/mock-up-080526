    The EU Artificial Intelligence Act comes into law today - which is relevant if your content is consumed in the EU, like all podcasts are. Article 50 requires clear disclosure if you use AI to “generate or manipulate” audio or video content. In an article, Production Expert clarifies that manipulation doesn’t mean de-noising or repairing audio (or using AI editing tools); but it does mean that clear disclosure is required if you’re generating whole phrases that a voice never actually recorded. Anything made before today does not need retroactive labelling. Fines can reach a maximum of around $40mn. (We’re not your lawyer).
