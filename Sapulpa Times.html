UPDATED: Back To School Bash, Bar Crawl, and GiGi’s Reopening: What To Do This Weekend in Sapulpa

Micah Choquette// July 31, 2026

Sapulpa’s best weather in weeks is hitting this weekend, and we have several great events lined up to get you outside and enjoying it! Here’s what’s in the calendar for this weekend.

Friday, July 31

Sapulpa Bar Crawl — 5–10 p.m. at six participating locations in downtown Sapulpa: Pat’s Place, CTX Coffee, Crossroads Cookery, Upstairs, James Calley Brewing Co. and Dough Momma’s.

  • Gypsy Ramblers Live — 7–9 p.m. at James Calley Brewing Co., 117 E. Hobson Ave.
  • Wyatt Justice Live — 8–10 p.m. at the Upstairs Speakeasy at The Cookery.

Saturday, August 1

5th Annual Community-Wide Back-to-School Bash — 10 a.m.–noon at 220 E. Lee Ave.

  • Sapulpa 2nd Grade Cheer Car Wash Fundraiser — 10 a.m.–2 p.m. at Ace Hardware in Sapulpa.
  • Game On! Ultimate Family Challenge — 4–7 p.m. at Sapulpa Nazarene, 302 S. Elm St.
  • American Legion Bingo — Doors open at 3:30 p.m.; early minis begin at 5:30 p.m. and the regular session begins at 7:30 p.m. at American Legion Post 36, 105 E. Lee Ave.
  • Groves & Trowbridge Live — 8–10 p.m. at the Upstairs Speakeasy at The Cookery.
  • Lady Chieftains Coed Softball Tournament — Time not listed; 1733 S. Wickham Road.

Celebrate the official end of summer with the Sapulpa Aquatic Center’s “Last Splash” event on Saturday. $1 entry after 5pm, with live music with Travis Kidd and Wimpy’s Food Truck will be on site.

Sunday, August 2nd

Centennial Sunday at the TeePee Drive-In!

Honoring First Responders and more! Bring the whole family and help us say THANK YOU while enjoying Spider-Man: Brand New Day under the stars! Gates open at 7pm. More information here.
