Yūichirō Umehara is a Japanese voice actor, singer, and musician. He is best known for voicing Goblin Slayer in Goblin Slayer, Seiya Ryuguin in Cautious Hero: The Hero Is Overpowered but Overly Cautious, Weather Report in JoJo’s Bizarre Adventure: Stone Ocean, Mars in Black Clover, and Keito Hasumi in Ensemble Stars!.

He is also the lead vocalist and guitarist of the Japanese pop-rock band Sir Vanity and has received several accolades for his outstanding work in the anime industry.

Early Life and Education

Yūichirō Umehara was born on March 8, 1991, in Shizuoka Prefecture, Japan, where he spent most of his childhood. He was raised by Mr. and Mrs. Umehara and belongs to the Japanese ethnic group. He has one older sister. He practices Shinto.

He attended university and, during his second year, enrolled at the Japan Narration Actor Institute (Nichinare), where he received professional voice acting training before making his industry debut in 2013.

Career

Yūichirō Umehara launched his professional voice acting career in 2013 after joining Arts Vision. His early performances in anime such as Merman in My Tub, Young Black Jack, Snow White with the Red Hair, Cute High Earth Defense Club LOVE!, and Mobile Suit Gundam: Iron-Blooded Orphans quickly established him as one of Japan’s most promising young voice actors. His impressive performances earned him the Best Rookie Actor Award at the 10th Seiyu Awards in 2016, marking an important milestone in his career.

His international popularity continued to grow through leading roles in Goblin Slayer, Cautious Hero: The Hero Is Overpowered but Overly Cautious, JoJo’s Bizarre Adventure: Stone Ocean, Black Clover, The Legend of the Galactic Heroes: Die Neue These, Darling in the Franxx, and numerous successful video games, including Genshin Impact, Granblue Fantasy, and Master Detective Archives: Rain Code.

Outside voice acting, he performs as the lead vocalist and guitarist of Sir Vanity, balancing a successful music career with his work in anime. In 2018, he temporarily stepped away from work after being diagnosed with acute disseminated encephalomyelitis but made a full recovery and resumed his career later that year.

Awards and Nominations

  • 10th Seiyu Awards – Best Rookie Actor (Winner, 2016).
  • Newtype Anime Awards – Best Male Voice Actor (Winner, 2017).

Personal Life

Yūichirō Umehara is currently married. He married his university classmate in 2016, and the couple has maintained their relationship away from the spotlight. There is no information about his past relationship.

Information about his children is unknown. Yūichirō Umehara, 35 years old, enjoys singing, playing the guitar, watching films, reading, travelling, and spending time with friends. He stands at 5 feet 8 inches (173 cm).

Net Worth

Yūichirō Umehara has a net worth of $3 million USD. His income is generated primarily through voice acting in anime series, animated films, video games, dubbing projects, live events, and music performances with Sir Vanity. His work on several internationally successful anime franchises, together with his music career, has made him one of the prominent voice actors of his generation.
