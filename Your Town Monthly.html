Free Outdoor Concerts in the East Bay This August

Soak up the long summer nights with free outdoor concerts in your community. Grab a chair or blanket, bring the family, and enjoy live music under the stars all month long.

FREE ADMISSION! Concerts are weather permitting. Dates, times and details are subject to change. Visit each city’s website for the latest information.
