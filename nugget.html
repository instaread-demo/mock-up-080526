Tournaments, OHL, Nipissing University among top priority ice users in North Bay

The policy includes ice allocation priority levels, cancellations, conflict resolutions, and ice management guidelines.

City staff rated clients based on priority. The highest priority is for tournaments and events, followed by tenants and agreements.

The city says the top priority level includes tournaments and events because they provide revenue generating opportunities to both the program organizers and the city, as well as, economic spin-off to the community at large.

The second priority level would be for tenants and agreements which would include the Ontario Hockey League (OHL) and Nipissing University Varsity ice sports teams, according to the 17-page document.

The third priority listing is city recreational ice programs, which include adult skating, senior skates, parent and tot skates, shinny hockey, club skating shinny and family skating.

Youth partner groups are next on the priority list. The group is divided into tier one and tier two groups. Tier One would include North Bay Minor Hockey Association, North Bay and District Girls Hockey Association, West Ferris
Ringette Association, North Bay Skating Club, Northern Edge Skating Club, North Bay and District Trappers Hockey Association, and Northern Ontario Hockey Association.

Hockey 4 Beginners has been grandfathered into this category due to its focus on affordable, introductory skill development programs for young children that follow Hockey Canada guidelines.

Tier Two would be not-for-profit groups, such as the North Bay Platoon.

Rounding out the list are educational and special need groups, seasonal clients, adult groups and commercial and non-resident groups.

The policy also includes a contract amendment period generally extending between Oct. 1 and Oct. 15.

During this time permit holders may request in writing a return of up to 20 per cent of their allocated regular season time without penalty.

“The purpose of this window is to allow permit holders an opportunity to adjust their allocated hours due to low registration,” according to the document.

When it comes to cancelling ice an administrative fee of $25 per hour of ice cancelled will apply to cancellations made seven days or more prior to the rental date.

City staff say this fee is necessary to offset a portion of the administrative costs incurred by the city’s arena administration office as a result of the cancellation. For all cancellations made less than seven days prior to the event, the full payment will be charged.
